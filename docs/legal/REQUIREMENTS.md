## Statutory requirements: KVKK

[6698 consolidated law, mevzuat.gov.tr](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuatmetin/1.5.6698.pdf) was requested but the browser source service returned an error. Recheck the consolidated text before launch. Official KVKK explanations supporting the implementation: [transparency](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/2033/Aydinlatma-Yukumlulugu-), [deletion regulation](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/5441/KISISEL-VERILERIN-SILINMESI-YOK-EDILMESI-VEYA-ANONIM-HALE-GETIRILMESI-HAKKINDA-YONETMELIK), [request procedures](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6938/Kurumumuza-Yapilan-Sikayetlerin-Usul-Sartlarina-Iliskin-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu).

Articles 4/5 require lawful, fair, accurate, proportionate, purpose-limited processing and a specific processing ground. Article 7 requires erasure/destruction/anonymization when grounds cease; lawful retention exceptions need explicit assessment. Article 10 notice identifies the controller, purpose, methods/grounds, recipients and rights at collection, irrespective of the ground. Articles 11–14 govern information, correction, deletion, automated-analysis objection, damage claims and applications. Article 12 requires appropriate security and processor oversight. Proposed grounds per activity are in AUDIT; optional birthdate/nickname necessity remains a blocker. No blanket consent checkbox was added.

The deletion regulation requires disposal records (at least three years), and, where a retention/disposal policy is required, periodic disposal intervals no longer than six months. Exemptions from VERBİS do not excuse erasure duties. Actual business retention periods are unknown, so no invented automatic purge job was installed.

## Regulator decisions and guidance

- [2026/347 official KVKK announcement](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8710/veri-sorumlulari-tarafindan-acik-riza-ve-aydinlatma-metinlerinin-ayri-ayri-duzenlenmesi-gerektigi-hakkinda-kisisel-verileri-koruma-kurulunun-18-02-2026-tarihli-ve-2026-347-sayili-ilke-kararina-iliskin-kamuoyu-duyurusu): privacy notices and explicit consent must be separate and understandable; a notice must not solicit processing approval or be treated as mandatory acceptance. Terms and marketing permission are also separate. [Resmî Gazete, 24 March 2026 issue 33203](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/03/20260324.htm) was requested but direct retrieval failed; decision and principle confirmed through the regulator's own announcement and principle-decision index.
- [KVKK cookie guidance, July 2025](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7353/Cerez-Uygulamalari-Hakkinda-Rehber): distinguish necessary transmission/requested functions from optional tracking; assess each purpose and processing ground. User-selected personalization is discussed separately. The present code contains authentication and requested planner/preference storage; no optional tracking was found. Inform and provide clearing controls; no artificial analytics/consent popup. Reassess long-lived functionality storage and real deployed CDN cookies. For future optional processing, block SDK/storage/network before consent, use equally prominent accept/reject/preferences, off defaults, granular choices, versioned evidence, invalid/expired-consent handling and withdrawal cleanup.
- [2026-10-01 request-response notification announcement](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/9024/ilgili-kisilerin-basvurularina-verilecek-cevabin-bildirim-usulune-iliskin-kamuoyu-duyurusu): answer as soon as possible within 30 days, preserve demonstrable notification and reasoned response. Do not treat a database status update as delivering a reply.
- [2019/10 breach decision](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/5362/Veri-Ihlali-Bildirimi): notify the Board without delay and within 72 hours of awareness; identify affected persons and notify them as soon as reasonably possible. Document delays and response ownership.

## Article 9 international transfers: statutory and operational

[Current official Article 9 explanation](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/2053/Yurtdisina-Aktarim), [official standard-contract documents](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7938/Standart-Sozlesmeler-ve-Baglayici-Sirket-Kurallarina-Iliskin-Dokumanlar-Hakkinda-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu), [signature/filing guidance](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8170/Yurt-Disina-Kisisel-Veri-Aktariminda-Kullanilacak-Standart-Sozlesmelerde-Dikkat-Edilmesi-Gereken-Hususlara-Iliskin-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu). [10 July 2024 Resmî Gazete issue 32598](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2024/07/20240710.htm) direct retrieval failed; cross-check regulation before launch.

Assess processing grounds plus applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, including enforceable rights/effective remedies, and correctly matched controller/processor roles. Foreign storage, support access and onward subprocessors all matter. Where using the Turkish standard contract, complete approved text/annexes, obtain valid authorized signatures and notify KVKK within five working days of completed signatures. Generic DPA, EU SCCs or provider terms alone do not establish a Turkish mechanism. The occasional-transfer exceptions are narrowly limited to irregular transfers; blanket explicit consent is unsuitable as a general basis for continuous foreign Auth/hosting. No country, signed agreement or adequacy finding has been assumed.

## VERBİS: conditional registration, general KVKK duties remain

[2025/1572 exemption announcement](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8388/KAMUOYU-DUYURUSU) confirms exemption for controllers with fewer than 50 annual employees **and** annual balance-sheet total below TRY 100 million whose main activity is not special-category processing. It also adds a separate exemption for special-category-main-activity controllers below 10 employees **and** TRY 10 million. [2026 filing announcement](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8752/2025-yili-mali-bilanco-toplami-bakimindan-sicile-kayit-yukumlulugu-dogan-kurumlar-vergisi-mukellefi-tuzel-kisi-veri-sorumlularinin-verbis-kayit-suresi-hakkinda-kamuoyu-duyurusu) addresses newly triggered corporate-taxpayers' obligations. Assess legal form, main activity, thresholds and all applicable exemptions; do not infer exemption from being a small website. Record evidence and revisit as circumstances change.

Do not impose a made-up universal IP retention period. Current consolidated provisions and changes need legal verification before marking this review complete.

## Recommended safeguards, not claims of statutory certification

Public legal routes without login or provider refresh; notices before data entry; local export/clear; verified-session account export; current-password account deletion; no secrets in exports; no raw error logging; no-referrer and nosniff headers; restricted provider admin credential; explicit launch gate; test-only deletion verification. These are engineering safeguards. Provider settings, organizational controls, contracts, legal bases and reuse permission require owner/legal review and evidence.
